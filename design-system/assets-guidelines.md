# 🎨 COSYlanguages Asset & Icon Guidelines

## A. Icon System
- **Library Reference:** Heroicons / Phosphor Icons outline set.
- **Icon Scale:**
  - `sm`: 16px (0.85rem) — Inline tags, metadata, tiny indicators
  - `md`: 20px (1rem) — Standard button icons, form fields
  - `lg`: 24px (1.25rem) — Section headings, cards
  - `xl`: 32px (1.75rem) — Hero illustrations, main badges
- **Stroke Width:** Constant 2px stroke width for vector clarity.

## B. Imagery & Aspect Ratios
- **Visual Style:** Bright, friendly, high-contrast, educational.
- **Card Thumbnails:** 16:9 or 4:3 aspect ratio.
- **Border Radius:** `var(--cg-radius-lg)` (20px) for cards, `12px` for inner media frames.
- **Lazy Loading:** All static image assets specify `loading="lazy"`.

## C. Logo Guidelines
- **Primary Logo:** COSYlanguages dice & speech mark emblem with brand wordmark.
- **Clear Space:** Minimum padding of `16px` around emblem.
- **Do's & Don'ts:** Do not distort aspect ratio or modify brand colors outside semantic tokens.
