# Data Sources & Attribution

All etymological data and language facts across the COSYgames datasets are compiled from reputable public reference sources, including:

- **Wiktionary** (wiktionary.org)
- **Online Etymology Dictionary** (etymonline.com)
- **Standard etymological and linguistic dictionaries** (Oxford English Dictionary, Trésor de la Langue Française, Diccionario de la Real Academia Española).

## Original Content Note

While historical and linguistic facts are drawn from these reference materials, all explanation texts, definitions, derivation paths, distractor options, and example sentences across `data/*/game_data.js` are original works created specifically for COSYgames.
